End of Arm Tooling

DC Tooling can design and build a custom EOAT for you.

Our services include:

  • Working with you to come up with the designs you are looking for
  • Creating 3D drawings for your approval before starting the manufacture process
  • Using quality materials and equipment to build your tool
    • Various colors of anodized aluminum
    • Pneumatic Cylinders
    • Vacuum cups and generators
    • Laser Sensing
    • Delrin or Silicone gripper pads
